Explainable PET + MRI analysis, in your browser

Choose an example subject or upload a T1-weighted MRI and FDG-PET registered to MNI space. The page runs a confound-aware model trained on hybrid PET-MRI from people with temporal lobe epilepsy and healthy controls, and shows why it decided what it did: region-level SHAP, comparison with an age- and sex-matched healthy norm, and left–right temporal asymmetry.

Your files never leave your computer: everything is computed locally in JavaScript.

Research prototype, not a diagnostic tool. Trained on 42 adults (OpenNeuro ds004054). It has not been validated clinically, it is not trained on children or on focal cortical dysplasia, and scores only describe similarity to this small cohort. It is a methods demonstration for a PhD application on early FCD detection in infants.

Methods and limitations
  • Data:
  • Preprocessing: T1 skull-stripped with a PET-derived brain mask, registration to MNI152 (2 mm) with ANTsPy; the same transform is applied to the PET. Intensities normalised to their mean inside a shared brain mask (MRI ratio, PET global-mean SUVR).
  • Features: mean value in regions per modality.
  • Model: L2-regularised logistic regression. Age and sex are regressed out of every feature (confound regression), then features are standardised. SHAP values are exact for this linear model (coefficient × standardised feature).
  • Healthy norm: for each voxel, controls' values are modelled as a linear function of age and sex; a scan's z-score is its deviation from that prediction divided by the residual SD. Ages outside the controls' range are extrapolations.
  • Limitations: small cohort; patients were younger than controls, so raw accuracy is inflated by age; affine registration only; no lesion masks or seizure-side labels; adults with TLE, not infants with FCD. Region-level importance is noisy at this sample size.
